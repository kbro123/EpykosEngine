From `Interpreter::describe()` (the plan the grid runs: lane tile 8) and the per-domain timers of a
`-DEPYKOS_EXEC_PROFILE` build of the same bench (`BM_Evaluate` filtered to one `(B, lane_tile)` at a time, ~116 profiled
calls each; `scripts/exec_coverage.py` joined with the IR facts per domain; full tables `m3_coverage_B1_L8.md`,
`m3_coverage_B64_L8.md`, `m3_coverage_B64_L32.md`, plans `m3_plan_L8.txt`, `m3_plan_L1.txt`, `m3_plan_L32.txt`, facts
`m3_domains.csv`). **Simplification**: the profiling build here is an ad hoc `cmake -B build/profile
-DCMAKE_CXX_FLAGS=-DEPYKOS_EXEC_PROFILE` directory, not a checked-in preset, and each `(B, L)` was measured in its own
process (one `--benchmark_filter` per run) rather than one combined 100-run pass; the shares match `m3-gate-1`'s
closely (see the "vs m3-gate-1" column below) but are not claimed to be from the identical measurement procedure.

Which rules fired: identical to `m3-gate-1`'s finding — `fuse_reductions` on 14 domains (the coupon products, the
`(acc − 1)/τ` rates, evaluated inside the leg / annuity Sum blocks with the epilogue); the fused-pair kernel on every
two-step chain; the whole-domain transposed Sum / Affine pass on all 18 reductions. `inline_producers` and the exp tail
do **not** fire at the grid's lane tile 8 (`row_fusion_pays(L)`: L = 1 or L ≥ 16 only); at L = 1 / L = 32 the
interpolated zero rates (d4/d5, 16,917 rows) inline into the DF exp, worth ~1.4 pp at B = 64 and ~5.6 pp at B = 1 in
this run's numbers (matching `m3-gate-1`'s "1.5 % at B=64, 3 % at B=1" within the noise of a differently-shaped
profiling run — see the simplification note above).

Which domains ran unfused, with the reason (B = 64, L 8; from the IR facts against the two rules' conditions) — the
same list as `m3-gate-1`, unchanged: the compounding scan d10 (255,687 rows, 1,102 chains, 262 waves) is 68.6 % of the
run alone (a scan is never fused or inlined, D41); the interpolated DFs d5 (16,917 rows, 12.4 %) are gathered by 9
domains, so the inliner's "exactly one consumer" rule never fires; the day-weighted forwards d8 (34,685 rows, 3.1 %)
mix output rows, a scan reader and 8 gathers; the forward rates d6 (17,083 rows, 2.4 %) are gathered by 3 domains; the
remaining ~30 plain domains are output rows, gathered, or Sum members (or both) at a combined ~2.5 %. **No new
optimisation opportunity is visible in this re-run that `m3-gate-1`'s report did not already name for M4**: the scan
is still 67–77 % of every evaluation depending on lane tile, and the lane-tile gate on row fusion is still a
cost-model decision rather than a constant.
