# M3 gate (G6) — the Stage A tape, fingerprint `d448afd70180`

Intel Xeon W-3223, 8 physical / 16 logical cores, Apple clang 21.0.0 (clang-2100.1.1.101), flags
`-O3 -march=x86-64-v3 -fno-math-errno`. Engine commit measured: `ee021a3` (branch `m3/m3-gate-1` cut from
`integrate/m1-m5` at `152d563`, the G5 landing; the commits after `ee021a3` are the results, docs and a script). The
machine-readable summary is `m3.json` (format `epykos-m3-gate 1`); the run file the perf gate keys is
`stage_a_stage_a.json` (D34, D45).

The problem: `blueprints/problems/stage_a.json` filled from the seed — 2,000 trades (378 seasoned), 70 quotes over four
curves (USD-SOFR 22, EUR-ESTR 18, EUR-EURIBOR-3M 18, EUR-EURIBOR-6M 12), 1,000 scenario lanes; the recording: 148 tape
inputs (70 free), 8,191 outputs (78 block outputs, 70 O1 knots, 8,043 O2 book outputs; no Select on the base tape, so no
O6 exports), 27,459,283 raw nodes → 517,036 after the E0 passes; the IR: 67 domains, 423,235 values, 5 scan domains of
1,107 chains over 255,959 rows, two DF domains (`exp(mul(@0,$0))` 177 knot-time rows, `exp(mul(neg(@0),$0))` 16,917
interpolated rows) both read by the residuals and the book, no DF computed twice.

Which rules fired: `fuse_reductions` on 14 domains (the coupon products `mul(@0,@1)` d16 / d26 / d37 / d42 / d45 /
d55, the `(acc − 1)/τ` rates d11 / d20 / d22 / d46, d18, d23, d34: evaluated inside the leg / annuity Sum blocks in member
order with the epilogue); the fused-pair kernel on every two-step chain (`sub(div(gat,gat),lit) -> s1; div(vec,col)` in the
forward rates d6, `mul(neg(gat),col)` in the DF exp d5, …); the whole-domain transposed Sum / Affine pass on all 18
reductions (16 of them with members evaluated in the block) (the `1 + f·τ` sums d9 of 17,076 rows in 54 length buckets, the legs, the annuities, the netting sets, the
book). `inline_producers` and the exp tail did **not** fire at the grid's lane tile 8: `row_fusion_pays(L)` is true at
L = 1 or L ≥ 16 only. At L = 1 and L = 32 the interpolated zero rates d4 (16,917 rows) are inlined into the DF exp d5 and
the exp becomes the pair's tail (`mul(neg(gat),col) => exp(vec)`); it buys 1.5 % at B = 64 (52.08 vs 52.87 ms) and 3 % at
B = 1 (1.522 vs 1.566 ms), because the run is the scan.

The scan is the program: 67–74 % of every run is the 255,687 compounding steps (2.2 M projected observation days of
7,537 compounded coupons after cse), evaluated as 262 waves of indirect tiles. The M4 items this measurement names: the
reverse of a fused scan step and a scan step fused with the `1 + f·τ` Sum it reads (d9: 3.5 % at B = 64, 4.2 % at B = 1) and the `(acc − 1)/τ`
epilogue it feeds; the telescoping rewrite where the observation weights equal the accrual spans (the plain-compounded
coupons: D43 rule 3, an exactness-class E1 rewrite); the lane-tile gate on row fusion as a cost-model decision rather than
a constant; the DF exp d5 read by nine consumers (an R4a "exp once per time" already, since cse merged the residuals'
and the book's DFs — the remaining cost is the exp itself, 12 % of the run).
